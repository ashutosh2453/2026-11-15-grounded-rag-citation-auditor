# Acme Robotics Warranty Policy

Every Acme vacuum robot is covered by a limited warranty of 24 months from the date of purchase. The warranty covers manufacturing defects in the motor, battery and charging dock.

The warranty does not cover damage caused by water, misuse or unauthorised repairs. Consumable parts such as brushes and filters are excluded.

To make a claim, customers must register the product within 30 days of purchase and keep the original receipt. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.

Batteries that retain less than 70% of their original capacity within the warranty period are replaced free of charge.
