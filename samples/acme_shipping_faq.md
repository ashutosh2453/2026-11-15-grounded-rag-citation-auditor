# Acme Robotics Shipping FAQ

Standard shipping within India takes 3 to 5 business days and is free for orders above 2,000 rupees. Express shipping delivers within 1 business day in metro cities for a fee of 199 rupees.

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are dispatched. After dispatch, customers may return unopened products within 7 days for a full refund.

International shipping is not available at this time.
